## About

**Bodhi Club** is the Quiz & Debate Club of LBS Institute of Technology for Women, Poojappura.

The club is a space for curiosity, critical thinking, discussion, knowledge, and healthy debate. This website serves as the digital home of the club, bringing together its events, committee, quiz league, announcements, gallery, and activities.

---

## ✦ Features

- 🏛️ Club introduction & history
- 👥 Committee & team
- 📅 Upcoming & past events
- 🏆 Quiz League & leaderboard
- 📢 Announcements
- 📸 Event gallery
- 📬 Contact & enquiries
- 🌗 Light & dark mode
- 📱 Responsive design
- 🔐 Admin dashboard
- ☁️ Supabase-powered backend

## 🎨 Design

The visual identity of the website draws inspiration from:

- Vintage academic journals
- University debating societies
- Scrapbook aesthetics
- Libraries & old books
- Editorial layouts

The design combines a warm, whimsical personality with a professional academic feel.
